Ticket viewer

A read-only view of your ticket notes: what needs you, every ticket, and a search across all notes.

  1. Click Choose tickets folder.
  2. Pick the folder that holds one folder per ticket, usually tickets inside your solutions-workflows folder. Picking solutions-workflows itself also works.
  3. Allow read access when Chrome asks. The page refreshes itself every few seconds.

Everything stays on your computer: the page makes no network calls and never writes to your folder. Chrome and Edge keep the live refresh; other browsers read the folder once and need it chosen again to refresh. Chrome does not allow choosing Documents, Desktop or Downloads themselves, only folders inside them.
